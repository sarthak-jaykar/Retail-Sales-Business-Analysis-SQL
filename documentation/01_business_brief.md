# Business Brief

## Situation
A retail business sells across regions, categories and shipping modes. It wants to know where it makes and loses money, so that marketing, pricing and operations decisions focus on the right products, customers and regions.

## Stakeholders and decisions

| Stakeholder | Decision | What they need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Executive team | Overall strategy and priorities | Overall sales, profit and margin |
| Regional managers | Where to invest and where to review | Sales and profit by region and state |
| Merchandising lead | Which categories to grow or reprice | Sales and profit by category and product |
| Pricing and discount manager | Whether discounts pay off | Profit by discount level |
| Operations lead | Which shipping modes to keep | Sales and profit by shipping mode |

## Business questions

| # | Question | Decision it supports |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | How is the business performing overall? | Overall strategy |
| Q2 | Which region performs best and worst? | Regional investment |
| Q3 | Which categories contribute most to sales and profit? | Category focus |
| Q4 | Who are the highest-value customers, and are they profitable? | Customer retention and discount review |
| Q5 | Which products generate the largest losses? | Product pricing |
| Q6 | Does a higher discount reduce profitability? | Discount policy |
| Q7 | How do sales change over the year? | Seasonal planning |
| Q8 | Which shipping mode performs best? | Shipping policy |
| Q9 | Which states generate the most profit? | State focus |
| Q10 | What should the business do first? | Priorities |

## Scope
- **In:** order lines in the secondary dataset, with the fields described in `02_data_and_quality.md`.
- **Out:** costs beyond the profit recorded, customer acquisition cost, inventory, and reasons behind patterns.

## Constraints
- Secondary public dataset, not checked against company records.
- Profit is as recorded in the data.
