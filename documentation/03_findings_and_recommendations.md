# Findings and Recommendations

## How to read this
Each finding follows: **finding → evidence → business implication → recommendation → measure**. The data is a secondary public dataset, so findings describe it and do not establish cause.

## KPI definitions

| KPI | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Total sales | Sum of sales |
| Total profit | Sum of profit, as recorded |
| Profit margin | Total profit ÷ total sales |
| Average sales per line item | Total sales ÷ line items |
| Average sales per order | Total sales ÷ distinct orders |
| Loss-making share | Line items with profit below zero ÷ line items |

## Findings

**F1. The business is profitable overall.**
$286,397 profit on $2,297,201 sales (12.5% margin).
*Implication:* the portfolio as a whole covers its costs in the data.
*Recommendation:* track margin by segment, not only in total. *Owner:* executive team.
*Measure:* profit margin by region, category and discount band, monthly.

**F2. West leads on profit, Central trails.**
West $108,418; East $91,523; South $46,749; Central $39,706.
*Implication:* Central earns the least profit despite substantial sales ($501,240).
*Recommendation:* review pricing, discounts and product mix in Central. *Owner:* regional manager for Central.
*Measure:* Central profit margin, monthly.

**F3. Technology leads, Furniture earns little profit.**
Sales: Technology $836,154, Furniture $741,999, Office Supplies $719,047. Profit: Technology $145,455, Office Supplies $122,491, Furniture $18,451.
*Implication:* Furniture sells well but keeps little profit.
*Recommendation:* review furniture pricing, discounts and costs. *Owner:* merchandising lead.
*Measure:* Furniture profit margin, monthly.

**F4. The highest-sales customer makes a loss.**
Sean Miller: $25,043 sales and a loss of $1,980. Tamara Chand and Raymond Buch are profitable.
*Implication:* high sales alone do not mean high value.
*Recommendation:* review discounts for high-sales customers whose total profit is negative. *Owner:* pricing and discount manager.
*Measure:* profit of the top 10 customers by sales.

**F5. Some printer lines lose the most.**
Cubify CubeX 3D Printer (double-head print) loss $8,880; Lexmark MX611dhe loss $4,590; Cubify triple-head print loss $3,840.
*Implication:* a small set of products drives a large share of losses.
*Recommendation:* review pricing and discounts for these products. *Owner:* merchandising lead.
*Measure:* product profit margin for these items, monthly.

**F6. Deep discounts remove profit.**
Line items at 30% or more discount: 1,393 lines, $362,770 sales, a loss of $135,376. 96.8% of them make a loss. Line items with no discount: 4,798 lines, $1,087,908 sales, profit $320,988.
*Implication:* discounting at 30% or more is the clearest profit leak in the data.
*Recommendation:* cap discounts at 30% unless a documented reason applies. *Owner:* pricing and discount manager.
*Measure:* share of line items at 30%+ discount, monthly; profit per discount band.

**F7. Sales peak in November.**
November $352,461 and December $325,294 are the two highest months in the data.
*Implication:* the holiday period carries a large share of sales.
*Recommendation:* plan stock and promotions ahead of November, and track against plan. *Owner:* operations lead.
*Measure:* November sales against plan.

**F8. Same Day shipping performs worst.**
Same Day has the lowest sales ($128,363) and profit ($15,892) of the four modes, on 543 line items.
*Implication:* Same Day may cost more than it earns.
*Recommendation:* review Same Day economics before expanding it. *Owner:* operations lead.
*Measure:* profit per Same Day line item against Standard Class.

**F9. California and New York lead on profit.**
California $76,381 profit on $457,688 sales; New York $74,039 profit on $310,876 sales.
*Implication:* the two states together carry a large share of profit.
*Recommendation:* protect service and stock in these states. *Owner:* regional manager.
*Measure:* state profit, quarterly.

## Caveats
- Secondary public dataset, not checked against company records.
- Profit is as recorded in the data.
- Findings describe association, not cause.
